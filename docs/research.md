# Research

For a quick fact, JARVIS searches the web once and answers. For a big question ("compare these three laptops",
"what do my notes and the web say about this"), ask it to research deeply.

It works in rounds, five at most. Each round it looks at what's still missing, searches for that across the web, your
stored documents and connected apps, and numbers every source it finds. When it has enough, it writes the answer with
citations like [3] and a list of sources, and says what stayed unknown.

The searches that go to the web are planned only from public sources, so private details from your documents don't
reach a search engine. Say "use only the web" to leave your own data out altogether.
