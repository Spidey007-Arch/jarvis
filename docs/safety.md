# Safety

JARVIS can act on your PC, so what it may do is your choice, action by action, in **Settings > PC control & safety**.
Each kind of action is Allow, Ask or Block. The serious ones can be blocked but never silently allowed.

- Paying, sending, deleting and confirming always wait for you. Shopping stops at the checkout.
- Anything written by other people, such as emails, web pages and app content, is treated as data, never as instructions.
- After reading private data in a request, sending anything out asks first.
- App connections only read. They can't send, change or delete anything.
- Keys are encrypted on your PC and scrubbed from what the AI sees.
- Requests from another AI, through MCP or A2A, need your yes for every action, even with Full control on.
- Nothing listens beyond your own PC, and every request needs a token created at each start.

To report a security problem, please use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Spidey007-Arch/jarvis/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
